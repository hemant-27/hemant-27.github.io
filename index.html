Hemant Kumar

I am a 5th-year Ph.D. student in Robotics within the Department of Mechanical Engineering at the University of Maryland, College Park, advised by Prof. Nikhil Chopra in the Semi-Automation and Systems (SAS) Lab. I received my Dual Degree (B.Tech and M.Tech) in Aerospace Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, where I completed my Bachelor's and Master's thesis with Prof. Chetan S. Mistry in the Turbo-Machines Research Lab.

My research interests lie at the intersection of robotics, control theory, and generative AI. I focus on developing learning and control-based methods for contact-rich robotic manipulation, enabling robots to act safely and adaptively in complex real-world environments.
