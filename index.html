Hemant Kumar

I am a 5th-year Ph.D. student in Robotics within the Department of Mechanical Engineering at the University of Maryland, College Park, advised by Prof. Nikhil Chopra in the Semi-Automation and Systems (SAS) Lab. I received my Dual Degree (B.Tech and M.Tech) in Aerospace Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, where I completed my Bachelor's and Master's thesis with Prof. Chetan S. Mistry in the Turbo-Machines Research Lab.

My research interests lie at the intersection of robotics, control theory, and computer vision. I focus on developing learning and control-based methods for contact-rich robotic manipulation, enabling robots to act safely and adaptively in complex real-world environments.

Research / Publications

I work on robot learning, multimodal perception, adaptive control, and trustworthy robotics, with an emphasis on autonomous disassembly, contact-rich manipulation, and real-time camera calibration for reliable robotic perception.

Toward Generalizable Robotic Disassembly: A Visuo-Tactile Pipeline for Screw Unfastening
Hemant Kumar*, Piyush Goenka*, Nikhil Chopra
Robotics and Autonomous Systems, 2026 (Link)

A visuo-tactile robotic disassembly pipeline for screw unfastening, aimed at generalizing across fasteners and real-world disassembly conditions.
